# Layer B calibration: back-test against PC National

Generated by `alicia/analyses/costing_calibration/backtest.py` from `data/processed/costing/parameters.json`. Do not edit by hand. Policy costing P4 (plan §6.3, traceability F-B-01 to F-B-05).

**Question.** Layer B turns industry productivity shocks into a long-run change in real GDP with a reduced-form closure (fixed employment, fixed rate of return, so capital adjusts). Does it reproduce the Productivity Commission's computable general equilibrium model, PC National, on the PC's own published simulations (*National Competition Policy: modelling proposed reforms*, 2024)?

**Formulas.** Wage bill W, value added VA, capital income K = VA − W, capital share α = ΣK/ΣVA = 0.476.

| Shock | Layer B | Naive (value-added shares, no capital response) |
|---|---|---|
| Labour productivity x (labour-augmenting) | Σ (W_i/ΣW) x_i | Σ (VA_i/ΣVA) x_i |
| Capital and labour productivity z (Hicks-neutral) | Σ (VA_i/ΣVA) z_i / (1 − α) | Σ (VA_i/ΣVA) z_i |
| Capital productivity k (capital-augmenting) | Σ (K_i/ΣVA) k_i / (1 − α) | Σ (VA_i/ΣVA) k_i |

**Result.** 6 of 10 target simulations are within ±15% of PC National's real GDP effect. Mean absolute error: Layer B 33%, naive 46%.

**Model-error band.** Across the target simulations, PC National's result divided by Layer B's ranges from 0.70 to 1.05 for labour productivity shocks (5 cases) and from 0.58 to 1.15 for capital and labour productivity shocks (4 cases). The Policy costing engine keeps Layer B's result as the central value and uses this band as the range of the step from industry shocks to GDP (interpretation A-P19; evidence entry `layer-b.model-error.backtest`). The largest gaps are shocks confined to health care, which is mostly funded by government: Layer B overstates them, as it does capital productivity alone in retail (B2), a shock type no channel of the engine produces.

**Notes.**

- *Weights.* Value added by industry is the PC's own 2018-19 database (`pc_national.initial_gsp`). Labour shares are from the ABS input-output tables 2023-24 (`abs_io.industries`, compensation of employees over compensation plus gross operating surplus and mixed income), matched to PC industries by name; Ownership of dwellings is IOIGs 6700 and 6701. The later-year labour shares are an approximation.
- *Closures.* D2 holds real government consumption fixed, unlike the other simulations, so a gap there reflects the closure as well as the weights.
- *Not back-tested.* Rent removals (B3, D4, D5, H2, NZ1rep), import-price and tariff shocks (B7, H4, NZ3, NZ5), output shocks (B9, NZ1agr) and the regional shock (H3reg, which needs regional employment by industry) are not productivity shocks in named industries. L1's state results in the workbook look copied from L2 (`gsp_gap`); only its national real GDP is used.
- *Use in the tool.* Layer B's weights in the Policy costing tab come from the latest ABS data (input-output tables, national accounts), not from the PC's 2018-19 database; this back-test checks the closure and the weighting rule, not those numbers.
