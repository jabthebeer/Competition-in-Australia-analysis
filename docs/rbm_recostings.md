## Differences

- **Non-compete clauses and other worker restraints, Option 2: partial ban (preferred).** Option 2's familiarisation takes 90 minutes against Option 3's 30 (Table 5) with the same people, rates and businesses, so it costs three times as much. The IA's small-business figures keep that ratio ($38.2m against $12.7m), but its medium- and large-business figures do not ($7.5m and $0.7m against $3.3m and $0.3m; three times would be $9.9m and $0.9m). The rebuild from the stated inputs gives three times Option 3's cost, which reproduces the IA. The IA's Option 2 medium and large figures look inconsistent with its own inputs.
- **Financial reporting relief for wholly-owned companies, Relief by legislative instrument instead of individual relief.** ASIC counts each company's $3,487 application fee (Corporations (Fees) Act 2001) as a saving. Fees paid to government are outside the RBE (RBM pp. 2, 4), so the rebuild keeps only the adviser fees ($1,513 a company): a saving of $18.66m over ten years. The excluded fees are the other $42.99m (12,330 × $3,487).

## Notes

- **Mandatory climate-related financial disclosure.** Treasury rounds each group's cost to $0.01m before adding four groups and subtracting the status quo, so its totals carry up to about $0.03m of rounding; the rebuild is matched within $0.05m.
- **Mandatory climate-related financial disclosure.** Group 3 under Options 1a and 1b has 228 entities, as Table 12 computes; the PIA's text says 278.
- **Mandatory climate-related financial disclosure.** Table 11 prints Group 2's transitional and ongoing costs as $755.89m and $412.99m, but its total ($618.03m) uses Table 9's $789.34m and $539.09m; the rebuild follows Table 9.
- **Non-compete clauses and other worker restraints.** The IA does not state the 1.75 on-cost multiplier, but its small-business figures need it: 925,758 × 20.2% × 1.5 hours × $77.88 is $21.8m, and × 1.75 is $38.2m.
- **Non-compete clauses and other worker restraints.** The IA's 73,403 medium and large businesses are split by CABEE's June 2024 counts; with that split the rebuild reproduces Option 3's $3.3m and $0.3m.
- **Minimum tax on discretionary trusts.** The IA's range for Option 2 links the restructure and election shares (10% restructuring goes with 90% electing); the RBM engine draws each range separately, so its range is wider. The central values are the same.
- **Minimum tax on discretionary trusts.** Non-business trusts' time is costed at the work rate ($91.54), as the IA does, because their advisers do the work; individual beneficiaries' at the non-work rate ($41).
- **Financial reporting relief for wholly-owned companies.** ASIC measures the change against no instrument, under which each company would apply for individual relief because the Corporations Act's reporting duty stays. The rebuild enters those applications as the current regime; the engine's no-instrument baseline would drop them.
