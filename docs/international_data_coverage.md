## Gaps

- **Employer business births** (`sdbs_births_deaths`): no data for Australia; ends before 2022 for United Kingdom (2018).
- **Survival to three years** (`sdbs_survival`): no data for Australia, Canada; ends before 2022 for United Kingdom (2018).
- **High-growth enterprises (employment, 10%+)** (`sdbs_high_growth`): no data for Australia, Canada; ends before 2022 for United Kingdom (2019).
- **Enterprises by size class** (`sdbs_size`): no data for Australia.
- **Quarterly company registrations (limited liability)** (`tie_quarterly`): no data for Canada.

Business births by ISIC section (the groups are summed from sections, because the European members publish no all-industries total). Sections B to S except O (public administration, outside business demography); among the core peers, agriculture (A) is published only by Canada and New Zealand. Missing for each peer:

- Canada: N77
- United Kingdom: K
- New Zealand: none
- France: none
- Spain: none
- Italy: none

Australia's business births, deaths and survival come from the ABS business register (CABEE), harmonised to the OECD's employer definitions in P2 (decision D-5). Its size structure also needs ABS data: the OECD's structural business statistics hold Australia only for 2007-2010, with all size classes together. Its quarterly entries are company registrations only; the peers publish those too, except Canada, whose quarterly entries cover all legal forms together.
