# Common industry groups: ANZSIC 2006 and ISIC Rev. 4

Generated by `alicia international concordance` from the ABS correspondence tables (<https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/classifications/australian-and-new-zealand-standard-industrial-classification-anzsic/2006-revision-2-0/correspondences/anzsic-2006-isic-rev-4> and <https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/classifications/australian-and-new-zealand-standard-industrial-classification-anzsic/2006-revision-2-0/correspondences/isic-rev-4-anzsic-2006>). Groups are defined in `alicia/src/alicia/data/industry_groups.json`; every class's group is in `data/processed/international/anzsic_isic4.json`.

## Method

The ABS publishes the correspondence both ways as HTML tables, one per division: each ANZSIC class with the ISIC classes it matches, and each ISIC class with the ANZSIC classes it matches. "p" after a code marks a partial match (only part of that class corresponds). Both pages are downloaded and parsed, and their pairs are checked against each other and against the ANZSIC codelist.

The groups themselves (``alicia/data/industry_groups.json``) are defined by ISIC divisions, the classification the international sources use. Each ANZSIC class goes to the group holding most of its ISIC matches: each match counts equally, since the ABS gives no weights. Data published only by ANZSIC division use the division assignment in the groups file, and the fit of each group's divisions is measured with the number of employing businesses in each class (ABS business register), so a mismatch that affects few businesses counts for little.

## Groups

'In group': of the employing businesses in the ANZSIC divisions used for the group, the share in classes assigned to it. 'Covered': of the employing businesses in classes assigned to the group, the share in those divisions. Weights: employing businesses at the end of the latest financial year (ABS CABEE).

Notes on the groups:

- **UTL**: ANZSIC puts water supply, sewerage and waste in one division with electricity and gas; ISIC splits them into sections D and E.
- **TRD**: ISIC has one trade section; ANZSIC splits wholesale and retail. ISIC also counts motor vehicle repair here, which ANZSIC puts in Other services.
- **ICT**: ISIC counts computer programming and consultancy (62) here; ANZSIC puts computer system design (7000) in professional services.
- **RES**: Real estate excludes the imputed rent of owner-occupied dwellings, which has no businesses or workers, and adds rental and leasing, which ISIC puts in administrative services.
- **PRO**: Two divisions merged, as the OECD often does (STAN M_N), less rental and leasing.
- **OTH**: Arts and recreation with personal and other services and private households. ANZSIC puts motor vehicle and machinery repair here; ISIC puts them in trade and manufacturing.
