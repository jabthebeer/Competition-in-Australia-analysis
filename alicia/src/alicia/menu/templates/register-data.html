Register data

How every law, requirement and barrier in the register is coded: its marks, the businesses it reaches, its sources and who administers it.

A working table. It shows the register as it is held, for checking. Every item is a draft from AI-assisted desk research until a person has checked it. To read about an item, open it on Legislative levers.

About the columns

Marks: each of the 8 drivers from −2 to +2; the total is their sum. Severity: a score from the coded attributes, for requirements and for the laws and policies that apply today. Class read from: severity, or the marks where an item has no coded attributes. Evidence steps: law named, law quoted, coded, counted. Businesses: an upper bound where the row says “up to”. Sources: the number of distinct links the item cites; “How checked” says what each quote was checked against. A row in grey is no longer in force.
